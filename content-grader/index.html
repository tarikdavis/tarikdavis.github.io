Content Grader

Compare human, AI, and hybrid content. Score it. Keep the winners.

A local-first tool for running structured experiments on content quality. Score versions on tone, accuracy, bias, and brief adherence, then export the winners as JSONL for training or evaluation.

Everything runs in your browser. No server, no telemetry, your data never leaves.
